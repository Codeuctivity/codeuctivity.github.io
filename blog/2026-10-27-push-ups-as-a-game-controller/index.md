## How it works

Prop your phone up on the floor, get into a plank, and start pushing. Every push-up lifts your animal; every rest lets it sink. Fly through the gaps between the pipes for as long as you can keep going. Collect boosts, unlock new animals and see how far your daily streak can go.

## Play it in your browser

No download, no account. Open the game, allow the camera and start:

**[Play Push Up Game](https://pushupgame.codeuctivity.cloud/)**

More about the game: **[pushupgame.codeuctivity.cloud/landing](https://pushupgame.codeuctivity.cloud/landing)**.

## Two players

Challenge a friend: each of you plays on your own phone, and you fly the same course. Whoever lasts longer wins the round.

## Your camera stays yours

The camera picture is only used to follow your movement, right on your device. It is never uploaded or stored.

## Try it

Warm up, prop up your phone, and see how many push-ups it takes to beat your own high score. We would love to hear how far you got.
